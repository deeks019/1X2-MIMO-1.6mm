# 1 × 2 MIMO CPW Fed Antenna (3.5 GHz)

This project presents the design and simulation of a 1 × 2 MIMO microstrip patch antenna operating at 3.5 GHz for 5G related wireless communication applications. The antenna system was designed to achieve high isolation, low correlation, improved diversity performance, and efficient radiation characteristics while maintaining compact dimensions.

---

## Objective

Design a compact 1 × 2 MIMO antenna system with good impedance matching, low mutual coupling, and enhanced diversity performance for operation at 3.5 GHz.

---

## Tools Used

• CST Studio Suite

---

## Design Specifications

• Operating Frequency: 3.5 GHz
• Antenna Type: 1 × 2 CPW Fed MIMO Antenna
• Ground Plane: On the same plane as the radiator
• Substrate: Rogers RT-Duroid 5880 (lossy)
• Substrate thickness: 1.6mm
• Copper cladding thickness: 0.35mm
• Application: 5G Mid-Band/High-Speed Data Transmission

---

## Key Performance Parameters

• Return Loss (S11) — evaluated for impedance matching
• Gain and Directivity — analyzed for radiation performance
• Radiation Efficiency — assessed for power utilization
• Envelope Correlation Coefficient (ECC) — evaluated for diversity performance
• Diversity Gain (DG) — measured for signal reliability
• Total Active Reflection Coefficient (TARC) — analyzed for active matching
• Channel Capacity Loss (CCL) — evaluated for MIMO communication efficiency

---

## Results

1. Reflected Power: 0.2% at 3.5 GHz
2. Absorbed Power: 99.8% at 3.5 GHz
3. Gain: 3.249 dBi
4. Directivity: 3.436 dBi
5. Fractional Bandwidth (FBW): 44%
6. Radiation Efficiency: 95.77%
7. ECC: 7.093 × 10⁻⁶
8. Diversity Gain (DG): 10 dB
9. TARC: -22.55 dB
10. Channel Capacity Loss (CCL): 0.00836 bits/s/Hz

---

## Design Insights

• Very low ECC indicates excellent isolation and diversity performance between antenna elements.
• High radiation efficiency ensures effective power transmission with minimal losses.
• Low TARC demonstrates good active impedance matching during MIMO operation.
• Low CCL confirms minimal loss in communication channel capacity.
• The antenna is suitable for 5G mid-band wireless systems, RIS illumination, and MIMO communication applications requiring reliable diversity performance.

---

## Conclusion

The proposed 1 × 2 MIMO antenna achieved strong impedance matching, high efficiency, excellent diversity characteristics, and low correlation between antenna elements. The design demonstrates its suitability for modern 5G wireless communication systems and advanced MIMO-based applications.
